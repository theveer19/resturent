Before you print

  • Scan it yourself first, with two or three different phones.
  • Keep the printed code at least 3 cm wide, or phones struggle.
  • Leave white space around the code — don't crop right to the edge.
  • Matte paper or lamination scans better than glossy, which reflects light.
  • Print on card and fold, or slide the tent into a stand.
