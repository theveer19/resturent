Our story

Made for big appetites.

YOLO is a 100% pure veg kitchen. We cook Punjabi dals slow, char our chaap in the tandoor, fold momos by hand and pull pizzas hot from the oven. From breakfast parathas at 8 in the morning to late-night cravings, there is a plate here for every mood.
